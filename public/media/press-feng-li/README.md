# Press — Feng Li / Louis Vuitton Fashion Eye: Paris

Street-photography documentation of Victor by photographer Feng Li in
Paris, 19 Dec 2019, plus the cover of the Louis Vuitton "Fashion Eye:
Paris" book by Feng Li (`paris-par-feng-li-fashion.avif`).

Only a curated subset is displayed on ABOUT (see `content/about.ts`,
`pressFengLiPicks`) — the rest of the burst is kept here as archive,
not shown publicly, to avoid a raw dump of near-duplicate frames.
