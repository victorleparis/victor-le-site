## Works / projects currently identified

### 01 — The Dinner Project — working title

**Status:** current / in development  
**Location:** Paris  
**Year:** 2026  
**Media:** Performance · Food · Fashion · Image · Sound · Film

Working principle:

> I invite 1–3 people for lunch or dinner.  
> I cook.  
> They wear my clothes.  
> I photograph them.  
> The encounter is filmed.  
> I create the sound.

The project is conceived as an artistic performance and recurring episode-based work, not as a conventional dinner or model shoot.

Each gathering may become an episode: `001`, `002`, `003`...

Possible episode content:

- date;
- lunch / dinner;
- number of guests;
- photographs;
- film/video;
- sound;
- clothing worn;
- notes / traces.

Working format:

- 1–3 adult participants;
- approximately 2–3 hours;
- hosted in Paris, initially at Victor's home as a zero-cost prototype for a possible future venue;
- Victor cooks;
- participants wear pieces from Victor's collections;
- Victor photographs them, potentially on film/analog;
- the encounter may be filmed;
- Victor creates or composes the sound/soundscape.

Participation and publication consent must be explicit and transparent, particularly because the first sessions may take place in a private home. Adult participants only (18+).

Potential pilot: around 10 sessions, mixing solo / duo / trio formats.

Possible future outcomes: exhibition, book/publication, film, sound work, Famous editorial content and/or basis for a dedicated physical venue.
