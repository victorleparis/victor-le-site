### Name as artistic material — VICTOR LE / LE VICTOR

Recorded 16 September 2026 following a conversation around the names Michael / Mikael and Arabic and Hebrew linguistic forms.

**Factual linguistic layer:**

- Latin *victor* means victor / conqueror / winner: one who is victorious.
- French `le` is the masculine definite article.
- Arabic `al-` is the definite article; it is not in itself a marker of nobility.
- Arabic *al-Manṣūr* can be translated approximately as “the victorious” / “the one granted victory”.
- Hebrew `-el` in names such as Michael is a theophoric element referring to God.

**Artistic association — not an etymology of the surname LE:**

Victor's public name accidentally permits a chain of readings and linguistic collisions:

> VICTOR LE  
> LE VICTOR  
> LE VICTORIEUX  
> AL-MANṢŪR

The resemblance between `LE`, Arabic `AL/EL`, and Hebrew `EL` is treated here as artistic material, **not** as evidence that Victor's surname has an Arabic or Hebrew origin.

This collision — victory / article / name / God / translation — may be developed later in writing, performance, film, publication or identity work.
